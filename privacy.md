# Privacy Policy — LFG System Bot

Last Updated: November 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data **LFG System** ("the Bot") collects and how it is used.

1. **Data We Collect:**
   - **Server Configuration Data:** Discord Server (Guild) IDs, configured channel/category IDs, custom role presets, and timer settings provided by server administrators via setup commands.
   - **Aggregated Statistics:** Anonymized daily and total counts of created and completed LFG parties per server.
   - **Temporary Session Data (In-Memory Only):** Active party rosters (User IDs and selected roles) and temporary channel IDs are held strictly in volatile memory (RAM) while a party is active and are permanently deleted immediately after the party concludes or times out.

2. **Data We Do NOT Collect:**
   - We do **not** read, log, or store user messages, direct messages, passwords, IP addresses, or personal profile information.

3. **Data Sharing:**
   - We never sell, rent, or share collected server configurations or statistics with third parties.

4. **Data Deletion:**
   - Server administrators may request complete deletion of their server's saved configuration and statistics at any time by removing the Bot from their server and contacting us on our Support Server.
